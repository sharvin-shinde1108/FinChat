# Risk Metrics FAQs

## What is Beta?
Beta measures a stock's volatility relative to the overall market. A beta greater than 1 means the stock is more volatile than the market, while less than 1 means it is less volatile.

## What is Value at Risk (VaR)?
VaR is a statistical measure that estimates the maximum potential loss of a portfolio over a specific time frame with a given confidence level (e.g., 95%).

## What is Sharpe Ratio?
Sharpe Ratio = (Portfolio Return - Risk-free Rate) / Standard Deviation of Portfolio Returns. It measures risk-adjusted return; higher values indicate better risk-return efficiency.
