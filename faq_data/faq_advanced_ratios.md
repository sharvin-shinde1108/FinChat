# Advanced Finance FAQs

## What is Current Ratio?
The current ratio is calculated as Current Assets divided by Current Liabilities. It measures a company's ability to pay short-term obligations with short-term assets. A ratio below 1 indicates potential liquidity problems.

## What is Quick Ratio (Acid-Test Ratio)?
The quick ratio = (Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liabilities. It is a stricter test of liquidity than the current ratio since it excludes inventory from assets.

## What is Interest Coverage Ratio?
This ratio is EBIT / Interest Expense. It measures how easily a company can pay interest from operating profits. A higher ratio indicates lower risk of default on interest payments.
