# Investing Concepts FAQs

## What is Diversification?
Diversification is the practice of spreading investments across different asset classes, industries, or geographies to reduce risk. The idea is that not all assets will move in the same direction at the same time.

## What is Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)?
DCA is an investment strategy where an investor regularly invests a fixed amount of money regardless of market price. This reduces the impact of volatility and avoids trying to time the market.

## What is Compound Interest?
Compound interest is the process where interest is added to the principal, and future interest is earned on both the original principal and the accumulated interest. This leads to exponential growth of investments over time.
